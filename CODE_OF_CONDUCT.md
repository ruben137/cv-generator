# Code of conduct

We want participation in CV Simple to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

Participants are expected to communicate with empathy, focus feedback on the work, respect different backgrounds and viewpoints, avoid harassment and personal attacks, and never publish another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, abusive, or harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
